Add an effect, or select an FX layer in Layers to edit it. Effects are layers: reorder them to go behind or in front of your characters.
To change Amount, Size, Wind, Opacity or the Emitter over time, open Graph, add keys, and pick the one marked (FX). Speed cannot be animated, because it would make the particles jump.
Drag a dot up or down to change its value, and sideways to move it in time. Tap a dot, then drag the two blue squares to bend the curve into the next key (this changes the timing of every property on that key, not just the one you are viewing). No squares? That property has the same value on both keys, or the easing is Hold. Tap empty graph space to jump to that frame (red line). On an FX layer, the properties marked (FX) animate the particle settings.
